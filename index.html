Backlog Browser for Epic Games · browser extension

Privacy Policy

This extension shows your claimed Epic Games Store titles alongside Steam review data and ProtonDB Deck-compatibility reports. Here's exactly what it does with your data.

✓

The short version: nothing you do in this extension is sent to us, because there is no "us" to send it to — there is no backend server. Every request goes directly from your browser to Epic, Steam, or ProtonDB, and every result is cached only on your own device.

What the extension reads

When you click Refresh library, the extension opens a hidden background tab to epicgames.com and reads your order history using your existing logged-in session — the same way the epicgames.com website itself does when you view your account. It does not see your password, and it does not use any login flow of its own.

What it looks up, and where

For each game in your order history, the extension looks up public review data from Steam (store.steampowered.com) and public Deck-compatibility reports from ProtonDB (protondb.com). These are the same public pages anyone can view on those sites; no account or login is used for either.

Where results are stored

Your order history and every Steam/ProtonDB lookup are cached in your browser's own local extension storage, on your device only. Nothing is synced to a server, uploaded anywhere, or shared with the developer. Uninstalling the extension removes this data.

What permissions it asks for, and why

PermissionWhat it's for
storage Cache your order history and Steam/ProtonDB results locally, so they aren't re-fetched every time you open the extension.
scripting Run a short script inside the hidden epicgames.com tab to read your order history using your own session.
tabs Open and automatically close that hidden background tab when you click Refresh library.
Host access to epicgames.com, steampowered.com, protondb.com The only three sites the extension ever talks to, exactly as described above.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting of any kind.
  • No advertising, and no data is ever sold or shared with third parties.
  • No account, sign-up, or login is required to use the extension itself.
